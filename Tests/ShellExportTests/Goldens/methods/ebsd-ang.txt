The EBSD scan (steel_map.h5, 200 × 150 × 60 × 60, scale 0.5 µm as stored) was analysed with Electron Microscopy Studio 2.4.0 (DiffractoKit 0.21.0, FormatoKit 0.19.0). The phases were austenite (Fm-3m, a = 3.595 Å; COD 9008469, CC0), ferrite (Im-3m, a = 2.8665 Å; Materials Project mp-13, fetched 2 Nov 2026, CC BY 4.0) and α-titanium (P6_3/mmc, a = 2.9508 Å, c = 4.6855 Å; Ti_alpha.cif). Indexing used Hough indexing (PyEBSDIndex 0.3.8) with band count 9 and pattern binning 2. Refinement used orientation refinement (kikuchipy 0.11.2) with method Nelder-Mead and tolerance 0.001. The mean confidence index was 0.82. The export shows the orientations, the phase of each point and the confidence index.
